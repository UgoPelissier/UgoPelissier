## Summary

PhD researcher in computational engineering working at the intersection of computational fluid dynamics, biomechanics, and artificial intelligence.
My research focuses on developing numerical and machine learning methods for the simulation and prediction of blood flow and thrombosis in
intracranial aneurysms. I am particularly interested in applying physics-based modelling and deep learning to complex engineering and
biomedical problems.

## Research Interests

Scientific machine learning for computational fluid dynamics and hemodynamics, including graph neural networks, transformer-based architectures and deep reinforcement learning for physical simulation. Current interests include intracranial aneurysm blood-flow modeling, clinical digital twins and personalized medicine.
